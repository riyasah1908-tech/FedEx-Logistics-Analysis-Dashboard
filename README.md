## 📌 Executive Summary

This dashboard provides end-to-end visibility into global shipment operations, allowing logistics managers and analysts to evaluate supply chain efficiency, cost drivers, and transit lead times across different geographic routes and transport modes.

## 🔍 Key Dashboard Features & Insights
### 1. 🌐 Global Shipment Distribution Map
* Visualizes shipping density and destination hubs across continents (Americas, Europe, Asia, Africa).
* Identifies primary logistics corridors and global fulfillment nodes.

### 2. ⏳ Delivery Lead Time & On-Time Performance Analysis
* Tracks transit duration trends against target delivery thresholds.
* Measures operational efficiency with a *61.26% On-Time Delivery Rate*, highlighting bottlenecks in international customs and last-mile operations.

* ### 3. 🚢 Freight Cost & Mode Optimization
* Compares freight spend across major transport modes (*Air, **Ocean, **Air Charter, **Truck*).
* Evaluates cost-efficiency by analyzing *Average Freight Cost per kg* against total package weight.

* ### 4. 🏢 Vendor & Supplier Performance Tracking
* Ranks logistics partners and vendors by shipment reliability, package volume, and average lead time.
* Enables data-driven carrier selection and vendor renegotiations.


## 🎛️ Interactive Filters & Slicers
The dashboard includes multi-dimensional slicing capabilities for granular drill-down analysis:
* 🗺️ *Country:* Filter by origin or destination regions.
* ✈️ *Shipment Mode:* Air, Ocean, Air Charter, and Land Transport.
* 📅 *Month:* Seasonal trend filtering.
* 🏬 *Vendor:* Individual carrier performance inspection.


## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* *Analytics & Visualization:* Power BI Desktop
* *Data Modeling:* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* *ETL & Transformation:* Power Query Editor
* *Data Sources:* Global Logistics & Shipment Records
